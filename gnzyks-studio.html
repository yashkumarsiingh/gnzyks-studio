Free Knowledge, No Catch

A few things worth knowing before you post

Captions aren't optional anymore

Most reels get watched with the sound off first. No caption usually means an instant scroll past, no matter how good the edit is.

The first 3 seconds decide everything

If the hook doesn't land immediately, the algorithm — and the viewer — moves on. Edit for the scroll, not just the story.

AI edits should be invisible

Good AI-assisted edits enhance a shot without making it look artificial. If people notice the edit, it's too much.

Consistency beats perfection

Five decent reels a week outperform one perfect reel a month. Editing fast matters as much as editing well.
